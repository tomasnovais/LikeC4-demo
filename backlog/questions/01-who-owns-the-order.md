# Who owns the order once it reaches the till?

- Type: open-question
- Status: needs-info
- Owner: Tomás Novais
- Affects: deliveryPlatformSystem.customerOrdering.orders; tillSystem.orders; deliveryPlatformSystem

The model treats the delivery platform system's order as authoritative and the till's as a copy. But once staff re-key an order, they often change it: a substitution, a discount or a correction. If the till then disagrees with the platform, which one is right, and who has to fix the other?
