# Registered names are the entities that run the software in the UK

- Type: assumption
- Validation: open
- Owner: Tomás Novais
- Affects: foodDeliveryPlatforms.uberEats; foodDeliveryPlatforms.deliveroo; ADR 0003

Each company on the model carries a name found on a public register or in its own terms. Two are less certain than the rest. Uber Eats UK Limited exists at Companies House, but nothing checked confirms it is the entity that runs the app for UK users. Roofoods Ltd is on the ICO register as the company behind Deliveroo, but one wiki-derived source calls it a former name. We use them because they are close enough for an illustrative example; check them before relying on them.
