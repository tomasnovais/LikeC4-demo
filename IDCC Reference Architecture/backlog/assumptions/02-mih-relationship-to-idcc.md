# The Manufacturers' Information Hub feeds product catalogues and is not an IDCC-built software product

- Type: assumption
- Validation: open
- Owner: Tomás Novais
- Affects: product catalogue system; Manufacturers' Information Hub software product; IDCC scope tag

Trade press reports the Manufacturers' Information Hub (MIH) proof of concept as UKRI-funded under the IDCC research and innovation challenge, mapping manufacturer product data to Uniclass, ETIM, IFC and the digital product passport dataset; no UKRI or HVM Catapult source confirms the link. We assume the MIH is a software product filling the product catalogue role (or feeding it), developed outside the IDCC delivery team, and therefore not IDCC-built. Sources: constructionmanagement.co.uk and digitalconstructionplus.com articles on the MIH proof of concept.

Confirm with the IDCC challenge team whether the MIH is within IDCC scope.
